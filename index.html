7am BTC Uploader

7am BTC Uploader is a private, single-user tool. It belongs to the operator of the "7am BTC Daily NYC Update" (@7amBTCDailyNYCUpdate) YouTube channel and is used for one job: uploading that channel's own daily video to the channel each morning, through YouTube's official Data API.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, no account for anyone else, and no other user. The tool runs only on the owner's own systems, under the owner's own Google account, and uploads only videos the owner has prepared.
